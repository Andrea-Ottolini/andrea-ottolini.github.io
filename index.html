I work on probability and optimal transport, with application to fields such as statistics, combinatorics, and economics[cite: 1]. I also work on quantum mechanics and associated problems in operator theory[cite: 1].

Prior to UAB, I was a Postdoctoral Scholar at Yale University and the University of Washington[cite: 1]. I received my PhD in Mathematics from Stanford University in 2021, advised by Persi Diaconis[cite: 1].
